Report an issue

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS/Android version, game version, expedition number and the steps leading to the issue. You may attach a screenshot.

Do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents. A PDA backup contains your entire campaign; share it only if it is needed to investigate the issue.

Keep your progress

Before deleting the game, keep a campaign backup through PDA Data between expeditions. On iPhone, Delete App removes local app data; Offload App preserves it.
