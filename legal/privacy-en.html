Zone Artifacts: Beacon

Updated: 2026-10-13

About this game

Zone Artifacts: Beacon is a single-player game. No registration is required. This version has no ads, real-money purchases or connected online analytics. The installed game for Android, iPhone and iPad includes its pictures and music and works offline.

Data stored on your device

The game stores your campaign, story decisions, items, coupons, unfinished expedition, settings and last 200 completed or abandoned attempts. Previous campaign copies may be kept to recover from a failed save. This data is local: the game does not send it to the developer. It remains until you replace or delete it. Temporary error codes stay in app memory and are not automatically sent anywhere.

Backups and sharing

You can create and share a progress backup yourself in PDA Data. The code contains game data and is not encrypted: only save or send it to places you trust. The system Share menu uses an app you choose, whose data practices apply separately. Device backups also depend on your operating system and its settings. The developer does not provide campaign cloud synchronization.

Deleting your data

You can start a new story in Settings after confirming the action, replacing your current campaign. To delete all local data, including language, settings and recovery copies, clear the app data in Android Settings. On iPhone or iPad, choose Delete App rather than Offload App; separately saved system backups are managed in your device settings. Any copies you saved separately or shared to another app must be deleted there yourself. For the browser version, clear the site data in that browser.

Permissions and security

The game does not use your microphone, camera, location, contacts or advertising ID. The audio system plays game effects and music; audio recording is disabled. Progress is kept in the app data area and protected by your device, but not by a separate game password. Protect access to your phone and backups. In a browser or Expo Go, loading the project and resources requires a network connection, handled by the relevant hosting service. This differs from the installed game for Android, iPhone and iPad.

Support requests

If you email support yourself, the developer receives your sender address, message and any files you attach. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate errors. Messages and attachments are stored by the support email service under its rules. Do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents. A PDA backup contains your entire campaign and is only needed if an issue cannot be investigated without it. You can email support with questions about the handling or deletion of information you submitted.
